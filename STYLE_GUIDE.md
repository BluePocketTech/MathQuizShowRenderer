# Loud Bookends, Focused Quiz Style Guide

## Brand Feel

This template uses a split visual system:

- **Intro and conclusion:** loud, opinionated, colorful, and arcade-like.
- **Quiz and answer reveal:** calm, focused, and optimized for reading math quickly.

The video should feel fun at the edges and disciplined during the actual thinking time.

## Intro And Conclusion

- Use saturated arcade accents: cyan, pink, gold, lime, coral, and violet.
- Keep the marquee frame, stars, glossy chips, and game-show energy.
- Copy can be direct and opinionated.
- The conclusion must include a key-concepts recap for each question.

## Quiz Portion

- Use a dark graphite background with subtle grid texture.
- Put the equation on a large high-contrast card.
- Keep answer choices flat, consistent, and low-distraction.
- Use one primary accent during the question state.
- Use green only for the correct state and muted grey for inactive/incorrect choices.
- Avoid confetti, marquee bulbs, heavy glow, rainbow outlines, and decorative motion during thinking time.

## Motion

- Intro and conclusion can feel punchy.
- Quiz motion should be limited to answer entrance, countdown, and progress.
- Transitions between loud and focused modes should crossfade or wipe smoothly.
- Avoid fast movement behind equations.

## Reuse Rules

- Future videos should normally change only quiz data.
- Add optional `keyConcept` fields to questions only when the recap should differ from the explanation.
- Any new quiz UI must preserve the quieter cognitive-load style.
